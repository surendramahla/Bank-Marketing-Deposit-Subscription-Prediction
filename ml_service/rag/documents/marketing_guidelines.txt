BANK MARKETING - STRATEGY & SEGMENTATION GUIDE
===============================================

CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION FRAMEWORK
---------------------------------

HIGH PRIORITY LEADS (Probability > 70%)
- Immediate action required within 24 hours
- Assign to senior relationship managers
- Offer: Premium Gold Term Deposit with bonus rate
- Channel: Direct phone call + digital confirmation link
- Script: Use confidence-building language, offer premium benefits
- Follow-up: Same day confirmation email

MEDIUM PRIORITY LEADS (Probability 40-70%)
- Action within 3-5 business days
- Assign to mid-level sales staff
- Offer: Standard Term Deposit + tax benefits information
- Channel: Phone call followed by email summary
- Script: Focus on stability, safety, and tax advantages
- Follow-up: Follow up call after 5 business days if no response

LOW PRIORITY LEADS (Probability < 40%)
- Low-cost automated outreach only
- Do not dedicate expensive phone time
- Offer: Information pack + digital signup link
- Channel: Email, SMS, or digital banking notification
- Script: Not applicable - automated only
- Re-evaluate: After 30 days with updated contact history

SEGMENT-SPECIFIC STRATEGIES
-----------------------------

RETIRED CUSTOMERS
- Key motivations: Security, stable income, preserving savings
- Messaging: "Protect your retirement savings with guaranteed returns"
- Objection: Worried about locking funds → Offer 1-3 month terms
- Best channel: Phone call (prefer human contact)
- Best time: 10AM-12PM weekdays

MANAGEMENT & EXECUTIVES
- Key motivations: Optimizing returns, tax efficiency, portfolio diversification
- Messaging: "Maximize your surplus with competitive fixed returns"
- Objection: Better returns elsewhere → Compare risk-adjusted returns
- Best channel: Relationship manager + email
- Best time: Before 9AM or after 5PM (outside core work hours)

YOUNG PROFESSIONALS (25-35)
- Key motivations: Building savings habits, goal-based saving
- Messaging: "Start building your financial future with a term deposit"
- Objection: Want flexibility → Offer ladder strategy (multiple short terms)
- Best channel: Digital banking, SMS, WhatsApp
- Best time: Evenings and weekends

HOMEOWNERS WITH HOUSING LOAN
- Key motivations: Financial security alongside existing commitments
- Messaging: "Build a safety net alongside your mortgage"
- Objection: Already have financial commitments → Show complementary nature
- Best channel: Phone call + mailed brochure
- Best time: 6PM-8PM weekdays

CUSTOMERS WITH PREVIOUS SUCCESSFUL CONTACT
- These are the hottest leads in the database
- Personalize messaging to reference previous positive experience
- Offer loyalty bonus rate (0.1-0.25% additional interest)
- Fast-track through verification process

CAMPAIGN PERFORMANCE BEST PRACTICES
--------------------------------------

1. OPTIMAL CONTACT FREQUENCY: Maximum 3 contacts per campaign cycle. More contacts significantly increase opt-out rates.

2. TIME SINCE LAST CONTACT: Wait at least 30 days between campaigns for the same customer. Customers contacted within 7 days have 60% lower conversion rates.

3. CALL DURATION CORRELATION: Calls lasting 5-15 minutes have the highest conversion rates. Calls under 2 minutes almost never convert. Very long calls (>25 min) suggest resistance.

4. MONTH PERFORMANCE:
- Highest conversion months: March, September, October, December
- Lower conversion months: May, June, July (summer / holiday period)
- January and February: New year planning conversations - moderate conversion

5. PREVIOUS CAMPAIGN OUTCOME IMPACT:
- poutcome = success: 3x higher conversion rate
- poutcome = failure: Still worth contacting but adjust expectations
- poutcome = unknown: Treat as a fresh lead

6. EDUCATION & JOB CORRELATIONS:
- Tertiary education + management job: Highest conversion probability
- Blue-collar workers: Below average conversion but not negligible
- Students and unemployed: Lowest conversion, focus resources elsewhere

AI MODEL INTEGRATION GUIDELINES
----------------------------------

The AI prediction model provides a probability score from 0-100%.
Use these scores to:

1. PRIORITIZE CALL LISTS: Sort customers by probability descending
2. ASSIGN RESOURCES: High probability → senior staff, Low → automated
3. PERSONALIZE MESSAGING: Use top positive factors from SHAP analysis
4. TRACK ROI: Compare AI-targeted campaigns vs. random campaigns
5. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT: Feed actual outcomes back to retrain model quarterly

COMPLIANCE AND ETHICS
-----------------------

1. Always follow Do-Not-Call registry regulations
2. Obtain explicit consent before recording calls
3. Be transparent about products - never mislead on interest rates
4. Follow GDPR/data protection for customer data usage
5. Document all customer interactions in CRM
6. Never pressure vulnerable customers (elderly, financially stressed)
7. Offer the option to be removed from marketing lists immediately
